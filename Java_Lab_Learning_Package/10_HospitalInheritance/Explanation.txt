[PROBLEM STATEMENT]
Implement inheritance in a hospital system:
- Common base class: HospitalPerson (id, name)
- Derived class: Staff (salary) extends HospitalPerson
- Doctor extends Staff (additional pay: patients * 500)
- Nurse extends Staff (additional pay: shifts * 300)
- Patient extends HospitalPerson (calculates bill = room + treatment)
