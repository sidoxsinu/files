FILE: ElectricCar.java

[PROBLEM STATEMENT]
Implement multilevel inheritance for vehicle insurance calculation:
- Base class: Vehicle (regNo, owner, value)
- Intermediate class: Car (premium = 5% of vehicle value) extends Vehicle
- Derived class: ElectricCar (battery capacity, battery insurance) extends Car
Compute and display total insurance premium.
