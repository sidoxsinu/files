[PROBLEM STATEMENT]
Implement hierarchical inheritance in a university staff system:
- Common base class: Staff (id, name, basic salary, calculateSalary())
- Subclasses extending Staff:
  1. Professor: Basic + 20% DA + 10% HRA + 5000 research allowance
  2. LabAssistant: Basic + 15% DA + 2000 lab allowance
  3. Clerk: Basic + 10% DA + 1000 transport allowance
Demonstrate method overriding and polymorphic salary calculation.
